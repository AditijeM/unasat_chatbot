## Institution Name
- English: University of Applied Sciences and Technology (UNASAT)

- Mission: Education focused on personal professionalization, effectively contributing to organizational success.
- Vision: To be the learning center of the region, leader in developing educational programs aligned with international trends.
- Slogans: "Education for Success" and "Striving for Excellence".

## Admission & Enrolment

### Prior Education Requirements
- **SOME**: HAVO with Mathematics and/or Economics, VWO with Mathematics and/or Economics, MBO (IMEAO – Business Economics or Commercial Economics or Business Administration), NATIN Business Administration.
- **SOI**: VWO with Mathematics 1, HAVO with Mathematics 1, NATIN with Mathematics.
- MBO level 4 diplomas (from 2012 onwards) also qualify.

### Foreign Diplomas
- Diplomas that give access to higher education in the country of origin are accepted if the country ratified the Lisbon Recognition Convention. Proof of Dutch language proficiency may be required.

### Entrance Examination (Instroomtoets)
- For applicants who do not meet the prior education requirements. Determines suitability for the programme.

### Enrolment Procedure
- First registration via the website; invitation for intake follows.
- Re‑enrolment starts in April; forms are sent by student administration.
- A student is officially enrolled only after full payment of tuition fees.

### Tuition Fees (College Year 2024‑2025)
- **SOME Paramaribo**:
  - Administration fee: SRD 2,000
  - Tuition: SRD 15,000
  - Licence fees (e‑books, Trajectplanner, etc.): €250
- **ICT**:
  - Administration fee: SRD 2,000
  - Tuition: SRD 14,000
  - Licence fees: €100
- **SOME Nickerie**:
  - Same as Paramaribo but deposited on separate accounts.
- Payments can be made in SRD, USD, or EUR (at daily exchange rate). Payment in instalments possible in agreement with the finance department.

### Payment Deadlines
- At enrolment: administration fee + 50% of tuition.
- Full payment must be completed before the start of the academic year (or before internship/graduation).

### Deregistration
- A student must notify student administration in writing. Deregistration before the end of November may prevent being charged for the full academic year.

## Education & Examinations

### Academic Year
- Starts 1 November, ends 31 October of the following year.

### Study Load
- 1 EC = 28 study hours.
- Bachelor: 240 EC (60 EC propaedeuse, 180 EC post‑propaedeuse).

### Teaching Language
- Mostly Dutch; some courses in English. English literature may be used.

### Examinations (Tentamens)
- Two examination opportunities per academic year per course.
- Results are published within 10 working days (written exams) or 20 working days (professional products).
- Grades on a scale 1–10, with 5.5 as the minimum pass.
- 40% rule: for multi‑part exams, each part must be at least 40% of the maximum score, and the total weighted score must be at least 5.5.

### Resits
- If a student fails, they have one resit opportunity in the same academic year (if available).

### Exemptions (Vrijstellingen)
- Apply to the Examination Board with proof of prior comparable study or relevant work experience.

### Fraud and Plagiarism
- Fraud includes cheating during exams, using unauthorized aids, impersonation, etc.
- Plagiarism includes copying text without source attribution, submitting work of others as one’s own, etc.
- Sanctions may include invalidation of the exam, exclusion from exams for up to three periods, or even permanent dismissal.

### Cum Laude
- **Propaedeuse**: obtained within one year, no more than 30 EC exemptions, average ≥8.0, all grades ≥7.0.
- **Bachelor**: completed within four years (post‑propaedeuse within three years), no more than 30 EC exemptions, average ≥8.0, all grades ≥7.0, graduation project ≥8.0.

## Internship (Stage)

### Duration & Timing
- 20 weeks, full‑time (4 days per week, 32 hours) in the first semester of the fourth year.
- Start possible in November or April.

### Requirements
- Propaedeuse obtained, all PP assignments completed, at least 94 EC from years 2 and 3.
- No tuition fee arrears.

### Internship Exemption
- Students with at least three years of relevant work experience at HBO level may apply for exemption. They must submit a work report and a portfolio.

### Supervision
- Company mentor (at least HBO level, no family relation) and a UNASAT internship teacher.
- Two company visits, interim and final assessment.

### Deliverables
- Internship plan (max 4 pages + COP).
- Mid‑term report (15‑20 pages).
- Final report (20‑25 pages).
- Competence portfolio (COP) with evidence of six competencies.

### Assessment
- Internship: 2 EC (mid‑term) + 7 EC (final). Portfolio & development assessment: 9 EC.

---

## Graduation (Afstuderen)

### Requirements
- All programme components completed (including internship).
- No tuition fee arrears.

### Graduation Project
- Individual research project resulting in a thesis (afstudeerverslag).
- Supervision by a UNASAT teacher and, if applicable, a company mentor.

### Assessment
- Thesis and final presentation assessed by the graduation committee.
- Final assessment (competences) during the graduation interview.
- Total 30 EC for graduation phase.

## Personal Professionalization (PP)

### Competencies
Six competencies developed during the programme:
1. Innovatief vermogen (Innovation)
2. Ondernemend gedrag (Entrepreneurial behaviour)
3. Besluitvaardigheid (Decisiveness)
4. Samenwerken (Collaboration)
5. Klantgerichtheid (Customer focus)
6. Ontwikkelingsgerichtheid (Development orientation)

### PP Trajectory
- Spread over the entire programme.
- Includes assignments (STARR reflections), portfolio building, and assessments.
- Final PP component: 12 EC.

## Rights and Duties of Students

### Rights
- Respectful treatment, privacy of personal data, clear information about education.
- Right to inspect graded exams within 10 working days.
- Right to feedback on assignments.
- Right to be heard before disciplinary measures.

### Duties
- Adhere to the Statuut and OER.
- Behave respectfully towards others.
- Use the UNASAT email account for all communication.
- Pay tuition on time.
- Follow house rules (dress code, no smoking in buildings, mobile phones off during class, etc.).

### House Rules (selection)
- **Student card**: must be carried at all times.
- **Dress code**: formal business attire (no shorts, sneakers, sandals, too casual clothing).
- **No drugs** on campus.
- **Computer use**: only for study purposes, no gaming, no pornographic/offensive content.
- **Photography/filming** only with permission.

### Disciplinary Measures
- Violation of rules may lead to access denial (up to one year) or expulsion.
